Prospects

Rank prospective customers by the two questions the business asks: who benefits most from switching to Hong Seh EV trucks, and who is the biggest account opportunity. Every number in the table is a model output from the same engine that drives the Results section; no heuristic weights, no adoption probabilities.

Each case is a full input set. The seeded cases are: one Illustrative fully-specified case (Evergreen Logistics) whose charging-site quote and verification evidence are complete, so it passes every gate and shows what a confirmed win looks like (numeric Total economic TCO and a positive saving); the demonstration 6-truck case; and documented Assumption reconstructions of the reference-HTML Yong Wen (5-truck ambient cohort) and Venus (3-truck replenishment) simulations. The Evergreen case is an illustration, not a real customer. The reconstruction cases stay diagnostic-only because their charging-site quotes are not itemised in the published source, so their Total economic TCO remains n.a. and they are flagged (not confirmed wins). 1. Decision snapshot

Live headline comparison of the four ownership paths at the current inputs.

All figures are economic total cost of ownership over the fixed 120-month horizon. Saving is measured against Keep ICE. A negative saving means the plan costs more than keeping the current trucks and replacing them with new ICE at COE expiry. "n.a." means the model cannot produce a number until the gating inputs (site quote completeness, per-truck input completeness, matching truck count) are supplied; it is never a zero.

4. Results

Four ownership paths at the current inputs. Keep ICE runs each truck to its COE expiry then buys a matched new ICE. All-new ICE replaces the cohort now. Plan A and Plan B follow the per-truck actions above. Recomputes live on any input change via the embedded engine.

MetricKeep ICEAll-new ICE nowPlan APlan B

Signed credits reduce cost. Vehicle economic TCO = current asset value + purchases + old/new operating + repairs + sale fees − disposal proceeds − vehicle grants − year-10 asset value. Saving % = (Keep ICE total − plan total) / Keep ICE total. 15% headroom = 0.85 × Keep ICE total − plan total. Max site TCO for 15% saving = 0.85 × Keep ICE total − plan vehicle TCO.

How numbers are computed

The engine is deterministic: the same inputs always produce the same outputs, with no network, no randomness and no external service. The fixed horizon is 120 months (ten analysis years, 0 to 10). Demonstration inflation and discount are all zero, so year-band escalation is 1 and NPV equals nominal.

Diesel visits        = ceiling(work km x available days x L/km / (refill litres - detour km x L/km))
Diesel quantity      = (productive km on available days + refuel visits x detour km) x L/km
EV grid intensity    = battery kWh/km / (1 - charging loss)
Public visits        = ceiling(public share x route/auxiliary grid demand
                               / (grid refill energy - public share x charger detour energy))
EV grid quantity     = productive route/auxiliary grid demand + public visits x detour energy
EV service / year    = (Hong Seh 200,000 km schedule total / 200,000) x EV km/year
Vehicle TCO          = current asset value + purchases + old/new operating + repairs + sale fees
                       - old-vehicle disposal - year-10 assets - conditional in-horizon vehicle grants
Site TCO             = initial installed cost + recurring cost + renewal + restoration - recovery
                       - checked charger grant   (counted once per plan)
Fleet TCO            = sum of truck-path vehicle TCO + shared site TCO
Saving %             = (Keep ICE total - plan total) / Keep ICE total
15% headroom         = 0.85 x Keep ICE total - plan total
Horizon              = fixed 120 months; demo inflation = 0, demo discount = 0

"n.a." propagates whenever a gating input is missing: a per-truck input status that is not Complete, a site completeness that is not Complete, or a path count that does not equal the included truck count. The model never substitutes zero for an unknown.
